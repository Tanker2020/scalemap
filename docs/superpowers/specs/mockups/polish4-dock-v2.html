WHY THREE SKINS

Each level of the app is a different machine: a planet of regions, a building of racks, a box of silicon. The dock should feel like the level it watches — its layout, texture, and motion all change, and that change itself tells you where you are without reading the rail.
  • Atlas — constellation header (real region nodes + one live arc, the page's single marching stroke), navy graticule, the world tree.
  • Floor plan — a real minimap: the SAME cabinets/pods as the floor, clickable (tap a pod here = select it out there). Hatched rack-rail dividers, capacity wells with U-notches, slat rows that shunt right on hover, one LED blinking (the busiest server).
  • Faceplate — a screwed-on name plate, PCB-dot body, a living vitals rail (cpu/ram/io + a 3.6s breathing pulse — the "it's alive" signal), drawers for config.

WHAT STAYS IDENTICAL

The scope rail (the way out), the tab names, the price color, the edit-lock law. Skins differ; grammar doesn't.

MOTION BUDGET

Atlas: 1 arc. Floor plan: 1 LED. Faceplate: 1 breath. Everything else is hover-reactive only.

The minimap earns its pixels: it's not decoration — it's the selection surface. At AZ scope the dock and floor share one selection state, so the minimap doubles as a "where am I looking" indicator when you've panned/zoomed the big floor away from something.

CLOSED ≠ HIDDEN

Every drawer's header carries its one-line readout even when closed — 8c · 32G, 2 rules, db ×1 — so the spine is a complete summary of the server at a glance. Opening is for working, not for seeing.

THE SAME SPINE, TWO POSTURES

Stopped: knobs, sentences, steppers — and the vitals rail idles. Running: the SAME drawers re-voice as gauges (readouts turn green and live, knobs freeze at their value with the live fill behind them), the pulse quickens, kill lights up. The shape never changes — only its temperature.

IT BREATHES

The one ambient animation is the vitals pulse (3.6s idle, 2.2s under load) — a heartbeat, not decoration: its rate IS a signal. Everything else moves only when you touch it.
